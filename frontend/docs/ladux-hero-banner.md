# LADUX creative storefront banner

The homepage hero uses an original sky-and-white 3D illustration: a pearl-white
laptop, flowing ribbon, soft clouds and a blue glass orb. The copy and action
buttons are real HTML; the illustration is a discrete image, not a screenshot
of the banner. The original purchase/catalog and consultation callbacks remain.

Asset: `../src/assets/ladux-hero-world.png` (1448 × 1086, transparent PNG).
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool; alpha is preserved.

Responsive layout: two balanced columns from 900px, stacked copy/art on smaller
screens. Theme rules are scoped to `.storefront-ui` in `src/styles/storefront.css`.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original premium 3D illustration for the RIGHT side of the LADUX laptop-store homepage hero, composed as one isolated transparent scene. No typography; webpage headline will be separate.
Primary request: design an imaginative 'your little creative world opens with a laptop' scene in sky blue and white. Beautiful, editorial, calm, cute and sophisticated. A cohesive art-directed composition, not a plain product cutout.
Subject: a slim unbranded pearl-white laptop open at about 105 degrees, seen in a graceful three-quarter view from slightly above, front facing slightly left toward the page headline. Precise elegant laptop proportions. Its screen is a luminous sky-blue portal with a sculptural white ribbon flowing out into a large smooth open loop behind the laptop, suggesting ideas taking flight. A few soft white cloud forms cradle a low oval ceramic platform under the laptop. ONE translucent sky-blue glass orb hovers near the loop and ONE small frosted glass tile with a simple four-pane embossed symbol floats in the foreground. Keep these as a balanced, coherent family of four main forms; plenty of breathing room. The laptop is the unmistakable hero.
Style: high-end 3D editorial illustration, soft rounded ceramic surfaces, restrained frosted blue glass, subtle pearlescent highlights, precise crafted geometry, ambient occlusion, silky shadows. Cute without a cartoon face. Calm premium technology brand visual with a sculptural, airy silhouette.
Palette: only pearl white, very pale ice blue, sky blue #38BDF8 and deeper sky blue #0284C7. No purple, pink, green, orange, rainbow or dark black. Blue screen may have a very simple abstract flowing white curve, no Windows imagery, no app text.
Composition: landscape approximately 4:3, scene centered and filling 85% of the canvas, balanced readable silhouette, all objects fully contained with a small transparent safety margin. Main laptop lower center, large flowing loop rising behind it; strong dimensional depth. Leave transparent gaps between forms. Ground contact is an airy soft alpha shadow, no room, no rectangular backdrop.
Lighting: large soft studio light from upper left, beautiful subtle reflections, bright highlights and soft blue shadows, no harsh specular glare.
Constraints: genuine transparent background with alpha. Entire scene is original. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no banners, no UI mockup, no device brand, no gradients painted across an opaque background. Avoid clutter, floating emoji stickers, excessive decorative particles, heavy toy blocks, plain photographic laptop cutout, exposed circuitry, garbled keyboard glyphs. Keep keys plain and understated.
```
